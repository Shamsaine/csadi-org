01 / ABOUT

Our focus

Better outcomes are
built together.

We work alongside communities to strengthen access to health information, education and livelihood opportunities. Our activities include health and nutrition awareness, support for routine immunization, and community action to help children enrol in and stay in school.

We believe lasting progress begins with listening. By working with families and local stakeholders, we help build responses that reflect community priorities and champion the rights and potential of women and children.

01Our mission

To work alongside communities for healthier lives, stronger learning and more secure livelihoods.

We focus on widening opportunity and championing the rights and potential of women and children.

02Our vision

To build Communities where every person has the chance to learn, thrive and shape a brighter future.

We see a future built through shared effort, local leadership and opportunity for all.
